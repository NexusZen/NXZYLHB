WHY IUTDS

Prestige

Be a part of Class

At IUTDS, we do not just debate — we do it at the finest stages imaginable. The finals of IUTIC, our flagship premier event, are hosted at iconic 5-star hotels. IUTIC 2025 concluded its grand finals at the magnificent Westin Dhaka, where every volunteer had the privilege of experiencing its legendary hospitality firsthand. Past editions have graced venues like Lakeshore Hotel, setting a benchmark that few university societies ever come close to. Being part of IUTDS means your debut on the debating circuit comes with an address that speaks for itself.

Culture

Be a part of vibrance

Life at IUTDS is a full experience. From the electrifying sparring of Donuts with DS to the high-energy rush of Greatness Express, there is always something to look forward to. Donuts with DS always brings the highest numbers of fresher attendees. During our festivals, the energy reaches a whole new level — think vibrant cultural nights filled with performances, laughter, and memories that last long after the event wraps up. Every semester brings fresh events, collaborations, and spontaneous adventures that make being a member genuinely exciting.

Perks

Be a part of exclusivity

IUTDS members enjoy a range of exclusive perks that go well beyond a certificate. Think curated gifts, custom merchandise, and recognition that actually means something. Beyond the tangible rewards, the society opens doors to a powerful professional network, connecting you with accomplished alumni, industry leaders, and fellow high-achievers from across the country. Whether it is securing an internship referral, finding a mentor, or simply knowing the right people at the right time, being part of IUTDS pays dividends long after graduation.
